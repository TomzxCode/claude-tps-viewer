Help - Metric Definitions

TPS (Tokens Per Second)

Total tokens (input + output) processed per second during a conversation turn.

ITPS (Input TPS)

Input tokens processed per second during a conversation turn.

OTPS (Output TPS)

Output tokens generated per second during a conversation turn.

Percentiles (p50, p75, p95, pMax)

Statistical measures showing distribution:

  • p50: Median (50% of values below this)
  • p75: 75th percentile (75% below this)
  • p95: 95th percentile (95% below this)
  • pMax: Maximum value
